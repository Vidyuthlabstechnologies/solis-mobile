You decide where
the audio goes.

Two engines, one switch. Keep everything on the phone, or let the practice turn on the cloud engine for its medical speech model.

Vendor API keys live on the SOLIS server, not on the phone.

Card photos deleted

Cards are read on the phone, then the photo is deleted. The full SSN is never stored, only the last 4 digits.

Consent on the record

Verbal agreement or an on-screen signature, saved with the time and location of the visit.

A real location

The visit record carries the real street address, never a placeholder.

05Who it's for

For practices without a
billing department down the hall.

Primary care

Office visits coded by new or established patient and medical decision making, with symptoms held until you confirm.

Urgent care

Scan the card at the door, check eligibility at check-in, and send the claim in one tap.

Specialty clinics

Your specialty and accepted insurers live in your provider profile, and procedure codes link to their diagnoses.

Nursing-home rounds

One account per patient, each visit with its own date and place of service.

Ambulance and EMS

On-device mode works offline, and the visit record keeps the real street address.
