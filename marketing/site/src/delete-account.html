Account deletion

Delete your SOLIS account

This page is for the SOLIS app on Google Play (SOLIS: Visit Notes and Claims, by SOLIS). You can delete your account in the app, or send us a request below if you no longer have the app.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open SOLIS and sign in.
  2. Tap your initials at the top right to open Provider profile.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Enter your password and confirm.

This deletes your account and sign-in sessions on the SOLIS server right away, and erases the patients, visits, notes and provider profile stored on that phone.

The demo account has nothing to delete on our server: it lives only on the phone. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, removes it.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted
Your SOLIS account: name, email, NPI, password hash and sessions. Everything the app stored on your phone, when you delete in the app.
Not stored by us
Patient records, notes and recordings are kept on your phone, not in a SOLIS database. In on-device mode audio never leaves the phone.
Cannot be recalled
Claims and eligibility checks already sent to a payer through the Stedi clearinghouse. The payer and the clearinghouse keep their own records as the law requires.

Request deletion without the app

Send the email address you used for your SOLIS account. We confirm by email from that address before deleting, then delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

Please do not include patient information.
