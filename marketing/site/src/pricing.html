Questions

Can I pay now?

Not yet. Billing opens with general availability. Until then SOLIS runs as a pilot: request early access and we reply by email.

Why a flat price instead of a percentage of collections?

SOLIS writes the note, codes the visit, checks the claim and sends it. It does not work your denials or appeals, so a share of your revenue would not be fair. You pay per clinician, and you know the number in advance.

What happens if we go over the included claim visits?

Extra claim visits are $0.45 each on Solo and $0.40 on Practice, added to the next invoice. You can also export claims as CMS-1500 data or 837P for your own billing system at no charge.

Does on-device mode cost extra?

No. On-device capture and notes are included without limits on every plan. The audio stays on the phone and works offline after a one-time download of about 150 MB.

Why is there no in-app purchase?

SOLIS is sold to practices. The practice signs up and pays on this website; the app only signs in. That keeps billing in one place for the practice manager.

Is there a Business Associate Agreement?

Yes. A BAA template is available for practices. See Security and compliance for what is in place today and what is in progress.

Is SOLIS on iPhone?

Not yet. SOLIS 1.0 is an Android app (Android 8 and later). iOS is not yet released.

Are CPT descriptions included?

CPT descriptions require an AMA license. The app shows short paraphrases, not AMA descriptors, and 4 of the 63 official coding rules that need the CPT license are not in the app yet.
