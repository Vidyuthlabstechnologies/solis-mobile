Get started

From this page to your first visit.

Set up on a computer or straight from your phone. Every step works on both. Try every screen with demo patients before a real visit.

Steps

  1. Request early access Now

    Tell us about your practice with the early-access form. We reply by email with the pilot details and the BAA template. At general availability this becomes a sign-up with email verification.

  2. Install the Android app Now

    On a computer: scan the QR code with your phone's camera. On your phone: tap Download for Android. Open the file and tap Install. If Android asks, allow installs from this source, then tap Install again. Android 8 or later. iOS is not yet released.

  3. Sign in, or use the demo account Now

    Tap Use demo account to explore with fictional patients and nothing on a server. When your pilot account is ready, sign in with it to use your own practice data.

  4. Set up your provider profile Now

    Enter your NPI and SOLIS looks it up live in the national NPI registry. Add your tax ID, specialty and the insurers you accept, once. At general availability the practice profile you enter on the website fills this for you.

  5. Download the on-device engine on Wi-Fi Now

    A one-time download of about 150 MB. After that, recording and speaker separation run on the phone and work offline. Downloads resume if interrupted.

  6. Try the sample card and a sample visit Now

    Scan the built-in sample card for the fictional patient Jordan A Rivera, then walk through a visit: consent, recording, the note, the codes and a test-mode claim.

  7. Your first real visit After the pilot paperwork

    Ask for consent, put the phone between you and the patient, and talk as you normally would. Claims stay in test mode until your payer enrollment through the clearinghouse is complete and the Business Associate Agreements are signed.

  8. Choose a plan and pay on this website At general availability

    Practice profile, BAA acceptance and payment by card or bank transfer, all on the website. The app has no in-app purchases. See pricing.
