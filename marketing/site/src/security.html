Security and compliance

What is in place, and what is not yet.

For physicians, practice managers and compliance leads. SOLIS is built for HIPAA workflows, and a Business Associate Agreement template is available. We describe the rest plainly, including the work still in progress.

In place today

On-device mode

Speech recognition and speaker separation run on the phone. No audio leaves the device, and it works offline after a one-time download of about 150 MB.

Keys stay on the server

Vendor keys never ship inside the app. They live on the SOLIS server, and the app reaches vendors through it. Cloud transcription uses short-lived tokens, so no long-term key is on the phone.

Encrypted in transit

The app, the SOLIS server and every vendor talk over HTTPS. This website sends strict transport security and a content security policy.

Signed builds

The Android app is a release-signed build. The SHA-256 of every download is published on the get started page so you can check the file.

Consent before every recording

A short script in English or Spanish. The patient agrees out loud or signs on the phone, and the time and location are saved with the visit.

Less data kept

Card photos are deleted after reading. From an SSN card, only the last 4 digits are kept. The full SSN is never stored.

Rate limits on the server

The SOLIS server limits how many notes and clearinghouse calls one network can make, and locks out repeated bad tokens.

BAA available

A Business Associate Agreement template is available for practices. It is a draft under legal review.

Where visit data goes

On-device mode
Audio and transcript stay on the phone. Works offline.
Cloud mode
Only when the practice turns it on. Audio goes to the Deepgram medical model for transcription; the transcript text goes to Claude to write the note.
Insurance cards and IDs
Read on the phone with Google ML Kit. Photos are deleted after reading.
Eligibility and claims
Sent through the SOLIS server to the Stedi clearinghouse (837P), with claim status from the same place.
Visit location
The real street address of the visit, or none if location is unavailable. Never a guessed one.

What SOLIS is, and is not

SOLIS is documentation and billing software. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or treat. It codes only what the clinician said, holds uncertain diagnoses for one-tap confirmation, and does not add vitals or exam findings that were not said. A note must be signed before a claim can be marked ready to bill.

There is no EHR, lab or pharmacy integration. Orders said in the visit are listed for the doctor to review and mark as sent.

We do not claim certifications SOLIS does not have. There is no government HIPAA certification; what a practice needs from us is a signed BAA and the safeguards above. Ask for the BAA template when you request early access.
