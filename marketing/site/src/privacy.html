Privacy

How SOLIS handles data

A plain summary for physicians, practice managers and compliance leads. SOLIS is built for HIPAA workflows, and a Business Associate Agreement is available.

Consent comes first

Before anything is recorded, SOLIS shows a short script for the patient, in English or Spanish. The patient agrees out loud, or signs on the phone. The time and location of that consent are saved with the visit.

If the patient declines, nothing is recorded and you can type the note instead.

Where the audio goes

Your practice chooses the engine.

On-device mode
Audio stays on the phone. Speech recognition and speaker separation run on the phone after a one-time download of about 150 MB. Works offline.
Cloud mode
Used only when the practice turns it on. Transcription uses the Deepgram medical model, and Claude writes the note.

In either mode, the note contains only what was said. SOLIS does not add vitals or exam findings that were not said, and every diagnosis links to the words it came from.

Insurance cards and IDs

  • Cards are read on the phone with Google ML Kit.
  • Card photos are deleted after reading.
  • From an SSN card, only the last 4 digits are kept. The full SSN is never stored.

Keys and vendors

Vendor keys never ship inside the app. They live on the SOLIS server, and the app reaches vendors through it. Eligibility checks, claims and claim status go through the Stedi clearinghouse.

Location

The visit record carries the real street address where the visit took place. If location is not available, the visit is saved without one rather than with a guessed address.

Subscriptions and payments

SOLIS Pro is bought through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; SOLIS never sees your card. To know whether Pro is active, the app shares your SOLIS account ID and the Google Play purchase record with RevenueCat, our subscription service. No patient information is sent to Google Play or RevenueCat.
