# SOLIS: Target audience, ads and other App content declarations

Play Console > Policy > App content. Complete Privacy policy, App access and Ads first: Target audience is locked until
those three are done.

## Target audience and content

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Target age groups | **18 and over** only. Do not tick any younger group. |
| Restrict Minor Access (shown when 18+ is selected) | **On**. SOLIS is for licensed clinicians and practice staff; minors have no use for it. |
| Could your store listing unintentionally appeal to children? | **No**. The listing uses app screenshots and the brand mark, no characters or child imagery. |
| Is the app designed for children / Teacher Approved? | No |

Who it is for (if a text box appears): licensed US physicians, nurse practitioners, physician assistants, EMS
providers and their billing and front-desk staff.

## Financial features

| Question | Answer |
| --- | --- |
| Financial features | **My app doesn't provide any financial features** |

Why: SOLIS submits professional medical claims (837P) and eligibility checks for a practice through a clearinghouse.
It does not offer banking, loans, payments, wallets, trading, insurance products, credit reports or financial advice.
The "Insurance" option on that form means selling or managing insurance products for consumers, which SOLIS does not do.
If a reviewer disagrees, the closest option is Support services > Other, described as "medical claim submission for
healthcare providers through a clearinghouse".
