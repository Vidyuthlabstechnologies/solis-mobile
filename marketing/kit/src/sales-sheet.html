Look at your patient. SOLIS writes the note.

SOLIS listens to the visit, writes the note, and builds a clean claim, on your phone. Made for physicians and small practices without a big billing department.

1

Scan the cards. Insurance card, license barcode, SSN card. Photos deleted after reading.

2

Consent. English or Spanish script. Agree out loud or sign.

3

Record. Your voice told apart from the patient's.

4

Review the note. Concise SOAP. Nothing invented.

5

Send the claim. Checked, then sent as 837P in one tap.

Codes from the patient's own words

  • "My feet burn at night" stays a symptom code until you confirm a diagnosis (ICD-10-CM outpatient rule IV.H). Uncertain wording waits for one tap.
  • Every diagnosis links to the words it came from.
  • E/M level by place of service, new or established patient, and medical decision making.
  • 59 of 63 official coding rules fully enforced, each citing its guideline. The other 4 need the AMA CPT license and are not in the app.
  • A Why button on every check: the rule in plain English, its official citation and the guideline text. Works offline.

Checked before the claim goes out

  • Against the code sets for the date of service: ICD-10-CM FY2026/FY2027, HCPCS Oct 2026, 2026 PFS, NCCI MUE and add-on edits, MCE v44.
  • Eligibility at check-in, claims in one tap and claim status through the Stedi clearinghouse. Export as CMS-1500 data or 837P.

Your practice decides where the audio goes

  • On-device mode: no audio leaves the phone; works offline after a one-time download of about 150 MB.
  • Cloud mode, only when you turn it on: the Deepgram medical model and Claude.
  • Vendor keys never ship in the app. Built for HIPAA workflows. BAA available.
