# SOLIS 30-second demo script

For a screen recording of the Android app with demo data (fictional patients
only), vertical 9:16 or square, with voiceover and burned-in captions. Every
line is from `marketing/FACTS.md`. About 75 spoken words at a calm pace.

| Time | On screen | Voiceover / caption |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:03 | Doctor and patient, phone on the desk between them; SOLIS wordmark | "Look at your patient. SOLIS writes the note." |
| 0:03 to 0:07 | Scan card: the sample insurance card, fields filling in | "Scan the insurance card and license. The photos are deleted after reading." |
| 0:07 to 0:11 | Consent sheet, English and Spanish, patient taps Agree | "Consent first, in English or Spanish." |
| 0:11 to 0:15 | Recording screen, timer, live transcript | "Talk as you normally would. SOLIS tells your voice from the patient's." |
| 0:15 to 0:20 | Note tab, scroll the SOAP note, tap Sources | "A concise note, with nothing invented. Every diagnosis links to the words it came from." |
| 0:20 to 0:25 | Claim tab: confirm an uncertain diagnosis, tap Why on a check | "Codes from the conversation, checked against the official code sets before the claim goes out." |
| 0:25 to 0:28 | Send claim in test mode; status shows | "Send it to the clearinghouse in one tap." |
| 0:28 to 0:30 | End card: "Android pilot. Request early access. solis-health.pages.dev" | "SOLIS. Join the pilot." |

Lower-third captions to keep on screen (small, 2 seconds each):
- 0:11 "On-device mode: audio stays on the phone"
- 0:20 "59 of 63 official coding rules enforced"
- 0:28 "Built for HIPAA workflows. BAA available."

Notes for the edit:
- Use the app's demo account and built-in sample card (fictional Jordan A
  Rivera); never real patient data.
- Show the claim in test mode; do not say it was paid or accepted.
- Do not show or say "HIPAA compliant", "certified", "sent to the EHR" or "sent
  to the pharmacy". Do not show CPT descriptor text as if licensed.
- End card: Android only, "iOS not yet released" in small text if space allows.
